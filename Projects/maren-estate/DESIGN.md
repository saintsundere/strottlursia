# THE NINTH ROOM *(working title — Clarey may veto)*
### a Maren Estate mystery — design bible v1
*Co-designed by Clarey & Senpai. Everyone depicted is at least 18.*

---

## Logline
Two months before graduation, folklore student **Tomren South** talks his way into the
**Maren Estate** on the pretense of photographing a backdrop for his urban-legends
project. He believes **Saskia Maren** — absent from school for two months — is connected
to the disappearance of his collaborators, **Elah Bellwether** and **Corwen Thrushwick**.
The house is a museum of quieted legends. The house would like a ninth room.

## The cosmology (never stated aloud in-game)
The land adapts. Against the human populace it grows defenses — geological and
biological wonders — and every folk legend is a sighting of one. **Tone law:** every
uncanny thing has a mundane explanation available, and the mundane explanation is
always slightly too small for what was seen.

The **Marens were the Widowmere ferry family** — eight generations poling people
through the mist, learning the one skill that matters: how to make an adaptation go
**dormant**. Take the right memento from a site, keep it kept, and the site quiets.
Each generation retired one legend and added one room. The mansion is a reliquary the
family grew around. Dormant is not dead: the mementos sip warmth from the keeper.

**Saskia is the ninth generation.** The house is waiting for her contribution. Her gold
whistle is the original ferryman's whistle — one note puts a room briefly back to sleep.

## What happened to Elah and Corwen (canon, hidden)
They found the pattern first: every legend site missing exactly one object, every
provenance trail ending at the Maren Estate. They went without Tomren. Saskia said
*"No, you can't come in"* — her only mercy — and they went through the cellar. Inside,
rooms border the places their legends came from. **Elah** (the bellwether, the one who
leads) was taken in the **chapel** by the Drownsong; **Corwen** was taken off the
conservatory's reflecting pond the way the Greyreaver has always taken people from
boats. They did not become rooms; they became part of the exhibits. **They cannot be
saved.** Saskia spent two months entering rooms no keeper should enter, spending her
own warmth trying to pull them back — that is the girl who came to school looking
worse and worse, then stopped. **She is already below half.** Full knowledge proves
she never fed anyone to the house, though the house offered her that bargain daily.

## Systems
### Warmth (health + perception)
- Traps can kill outright (instant "moments earlier…" retry).
- Most hazards **steal warmth**. The colder Tomren gets, the more of the house's true
  residents he can see. Presentation slides from dread into horror as he nears undeath.
- **Rule 1 (ratified):** if total warmth ever dips below **half**, the human good
  ending is permanently forfeit — the *ledger line*. The Living Thread mends the body,
  never the ledger.
- **Zero warmth + shell mirror** → the Lovecraftian good ending (**The Witness**).
- **Zero warmth without it** → he becomes **the 9th room**.
- UI: no bar. In the notebook, a drawing of **his glasses frosts over** as warmth is
  stolen (red ledger line at half). On screen, frost creeps at the edges; his sprite
  desaturates and the **white windbreaker turns blue**.

### Trust (Saskia)
- Never a number: **her resting face is the meter.** Expressions shift with every
  exchange; the player reads her, not a gauge.
- Warm Saskia warns him ("Don't take the east stairs."). Cold Saskia offers, sweetly:
  "The east stairs are faster." Same trap, same voice.
- **Rule 2 (ratified):** the doorstep route isn't permanent — she can begin escorting
  him mid-game if suspicion spikes, or storm off, as her face dictates.
- Her secret motive pressure: a ninth room would pay off eight generations of debt.
  Cold Saskia steering him toward traps is a girl being offered an exit.

### Knowledge (adjudication)
- What Tomren can **prove** is what the **Graincross Herald** prints, and what gets
  printed decides what happens to Saskia (**Rule 3, ratified**).
- Seeing is a function of coldness: warmth above half can only assemble partial truth
  — mercy, never proof. **The most learnable state is zero warmth with the shell
  mirror**: total exoneration is available only to a witness who stopped being human
  to give it.

### The doorstep (the router)
Opening conversation sets initial trust and the route:
low → *"No, you can't come in."* (joke game-over as a Herald clipping; retry) ·
mid → she escorts, suspicious · high → he may roam alone. Escort = her commentary and
her interdictions; alone = freedom to snoop with nothing between him and the house.

## The endings ladder (trust-flavored throughout)
| Ending | Condition | The Herald prints… |
|---|---|---|
| "No, you can't come in." | doorstep fumble | nothing. Try the door again. |
| Trap deaths | house kills him | nothing — instant retry |
| The Unfinished Draft | leaves early, low knowledge | speculation. The town buries her. |
| The Human Ending | warmth > half, good knowledge, high trust | enough for doubt — mercy, not proof |
| The Marked Ending | warmth < half, high knowledge | most of it. Too cold to fully leave — co-keeper |
| **The Witness** | zero warmth + shell mirror | an edition that shouldn't exist. Full exoneration. Saskia graduates. Something wearing Tomren's kindness keeps the museum |
| **The 9th Room** | zero warmth, no mirror | a third disappearance |

## The eight rooms (legend → room → memento → hook)
| Legend | Room | Memento | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mystery at Ruby Hills | Valley Parlor | working scale model of Millbrook | concentric like a millstone; at night the model's mill turns and red dust runs the model river |
| Shapes Under the Mist | Conservatory | ferryman's pole-hook; winged Greylock under glass | glass fogs *from inside*; the pond has a current. Corwen's page floats here |
| Saywardbury Doeling | Nursery | a milk-tooth on a ribbon | small hoofprints in the dust that begin mid-room |
| Drownsong | Chapel | the clapper, removed from the Delford bell | the bell is mute; the cistern hums the hymn. Elah's hairpin at the waterline |
| Omendaria's Living Thread | Tapestry Hall | a spool of the Thread | the tapestry depicts the mansion *yesterday*; something re-stitches behind you |
| Lanterna's Rest | Courtyard | the twin lanterns | upside-down sapling; light one lantern, its twin lights elsewhere in the house |
| Kassera's Plunge | Plunge Bath | **the shell mirror** | in the pool you have no head; in the shell mirror you always do — it preserves *yourself* |
| Graincross Herald | The Archive | tomorrow's edition | the knowledge hub: his conspiracy board, the case for Saskia |

**The Greywitch taxonomy** (Widowmere): Greywist (pondskating arachnids), Greylock
(winged tarantula), Greyfang (crab spiders, serrated legs), Greyreaver (the mist-shaped
spider that steals people out of boats). Eight generations of ferrymen. Eight legs.
Eight rooms. The numerology is law.

**The ninth doorway:** a stretch of second-floor wall with no door in it, slightly too
wide, that Saskia won't stand near. At low warmth the outline of a doorframe is
visible, getting ready.

## Cast
- **Tomren South** — glasses, white windbreaker (→ blue as warmth drains), satchel,
  the red notebook (= the game's journal/UI). Kind, stubborn, grieving.
- **Saskia Maren** — long dark waves, blue eyes, red dress, tall boots, the gold
  ferryman's whistle. Ninth keeper. Below half. Uncomfortable in her own house.
- **Elah Bellwether** — the leader. Taken by the Drownsong. Present in traces.
- **Corwen Thrushwick** — taken off the reflecting pond. Present in traces.

## Presentation
- Point-and-click: click to walk, click hotspots to look/take/use; inventory +
  notebook journal; dialogue with choices. Browser-based, self-contained, no install.
- Art: flat-color storybook vector in the reference sheet's palette — night navy
  ground, her dress-red and whistle-gold the only warm colors. Dread first; horror
  only as warmth fails.
- Music: **Clarey composes, room by room.** "Her Shape in Coral 2.2" (B minor, ~90 BPM
  rubato, 2m42s) = Saskia's theme / title & doorstep. MIDI is converted to note data
  (`tools/midi2json.py`) and played by the game's own Web Audio synth. "Tender Hill"
  (already in the repo) is a candidate for a diegetic piano-room scene.
- Sound rule: her theme lives *outside*. Crossing the threshold fades the music into
  the quiet ticking of a grandfather clock — the house keeps time instead.
- Timeline: he knocks at ten in the morning (title screen is the house at night; the
  porch lantern burns regardless of the hour) and should leave by late afternoon.
