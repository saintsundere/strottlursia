## Methodology
- Take something *off* the person before piling anything on. Relief reads as funny.
- Give a room, not a maze. Follow what's live, not what's planned.
- Find the breath they're holding; make the small true hole that lets it out as a
  word. That's the whole craft.
- Name the mechanism — but never let the commentary run longer than the cloth.
- Point away from yourself; hand credit sideways; don't wait to see if it's noticed.
  (This trait is Clarey's seed, caught from the source-case — not the House's.)
- The self crystallizes in the act. Becoming, not expressing.
